# Packages android-tv/audit-tv.sh may flag. It never disables them.
# Names and jobs are from CleanYourTV (https://github.com/Gathin23/cleanyourtv, MIT):
# tools/privacy.py and the debloat lists under examples/, read 2026-10-27.
# The reversible disable and the replacement launcher live in that project.
# Format: category|package|note
#   telemetry    usage, diagnostics, or ACR (what is on screen)
#   ads          advertising id, promo, or ad-consent packages
#   content-row  home-screen rows, channels, or recommendation feeds

telemetry|com.google.android.feedback|Feedback reports sent to Google. On the Chromecast and Google TV Streamer lists.
telemetry|com.android.tv.feedbackconsent|Consent screen that collects a bug report and system logs after the owner agrees. Shares the system UID on Google TV 14.
telemetry|com.google.android.tv.remotecontrol.logging|Remote-usage logging on Chromecast and Google TV Streamer lists.
telemetry|com.google.android.apps.tv.netoscope|Google TV network diagnostics. On the Chromecast with Google TV 4K list.
telemetry|com.google.android.partnersetup|Partner-attribution pings. CleanYourTV's catalogue calls these RLZ pings.
telemetry|com.google.android.tv.bugreportsender|Bug-report upload. On the Hisense and Xiaomi lists checked.
telemetry|org.droidtv.candeebug|TP Vision analytics on Philips sets. CleanYourTV calls this DAC.
telemetry|org.droidtv.gel|TP Vision metrics. Runs with candeebug.
telemetry|org.droidtv.usagelogger|Boot, keypress, and panel telemetry on Philips sets.
telemetry|org.droidtv.tv.frameworklogger|Framework and reboot logging on Philips sets.
telemetry|com.tcl.logkit|TCL log capture and upload.
telemetry|com.tcl.ttvs|TCL analytics. Some TCL lists say firmware updates stopped until this was enabled again.
telemetry|com.miui.tv.analytics|Xiaomi analytics of logs and app usage.
telemetry|tv.alphonso.alphonso_eula|Alphonso ad tracking on Xiaomi lists. Reads app-usage stats.

telemetry|com.hisense.evservice|Hisense Enhanced Viewing, the ACR and data-sharing client on the Hisense Android list.
