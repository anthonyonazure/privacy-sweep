# Household checklists

privacy-sweep's scripts reach computers and Android devices. Most of what a home holds
(speakers, cameras, cars, consoles, printers, accounts) cannot be reached by a script, so
these pages are checklists you work through by hand. Nothing here runs anything.

Menu names change between app versions. If a name below does not match your screen, look
for the nearest match under Privacy or Settings.

## Order that pays off most

1. **Accounts** ([accounts.md](accounts.md)). The company keeps the history, not the device.
   Clearing a device does not clear the account behind it.
2. **Cameras, speakers, and robot vacuums** ([smart-home.md](smart-home.md)). They record
   inside your home and keep it in a cloud.
3. **Cars and wearables** ([cars-and-wearables.md](cars-and-wearables.md)).
4. **Consoles, printers, drives, and retiring devices**
   ([consoles-printers-drives-retiring.md](consoles-printers-drives-retiring.md)).

## Three rules that apply everywhere

- **A factory reset wipes the device, not your account.** Cloud copies, subscriptions, and
  the device's entry in your account stay until you remove them there.
- **Remove the device from your account before you sell, give away, or recycle it.**
- **Deleting an account is not the same as deleting what is in it.** Check what the
  company says it keeps after deletion.

## What is marked "check first"

Where a step could not be confirmed, the page says so. Open the settings screen and look
before you rely on it.
