# Accounts: where the history really lives

Clearing a phone or computer does not clear what Google, Apple, or Microsoft hold. Work
through each account you use.

## Google

- **Web and App Activity:** location and IP details drop after 30 days. Set auto-delete at
  myaccount.google.com, Data and privacy, History settings.
- **Timeline:** off by default, now stored on the device, and only the Maps phone app
  feeds it. New Timelines auto-delete after 3 months.
- **Devices:** sign out devices you no longer use.
- **Connected apps:** remove apps and sites you no longer use.
- **Results about you:** removes your details from Google Search results only, not from
  the site that published them.
- **Backup codes:** you get 10, each single use. Store them somewhere safe.

## Apple

- **Safety Check:** Settings, Privacy and Security, Safety Check. It shows who and what
  has access, and can reset it.
- **iCloud Backup:** turning it off keeps the last backup for 180 days.
- **Advanced Data Protection:** encrypts 25 categories end to end (not Mail, Contacts, or
  Calendars). It needs a recovery contact or key, so set that up first.

## Microsoft

- **Privacy dashboard:** account.microsoft.com/privacy. Clearing there does not clear the
  device.
- **Recall:** off by default. If you turned it on, remove it under Settings, Privacy and
  security, Recall and snapshots.

## Quiet mail forwarding

In Gmail and Outlook, a forwarding rule or filter can copy your mail to someone else
without any visible sign. Gmail's notice shows only for the first week. Open the
forwarding and filter lists and remove anything you did not set up.

## Data brokers and credit

- **People-search sites:** opting out is free, one site at a time. Paid removal services
  were found largely ineffective. Manual opt-outs worked for about 70% of requests within
  a week.
- **California residents:** the state's DROP tool (privacy.ca.gov/drop) sends one request
  to more than 600 registered brokers.
- **Credit freeze:** free at all three bureaus.
- **IRS Identity Protection PIN:** renews every year.

## Not covered (check first)

Instagram, X, and LinkedIn menu wording, password manager audit steps, the Google Security
Checkup page, and each credit bureau's freeze page.
