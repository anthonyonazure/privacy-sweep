# Smart speakers, cameras, and robot vacuums

## Voice assistants

| Device | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Amazon Alexa | In the Alexa app: More, Alexa Privacy, Review Voice History. Delete there. The "Do Not Send Voice Recordings" setting ended in March 2025, so recordings now go to Amazon. Resetting or deregistering a speaker does not delete recordings. Alexa+ may change how voice deletion works (check first). |
| Google Nest and Gemini for Home | In the Home app: Privacy, Home History. History auto-deletes after 18 months by default, and you can choose 3, 18, or 36. Settings names may have changed with Gemini for Home (check first). |
| Apple Siri | Audio is not kept unless you opt in. Transcripts can be kept up to two years under a random ID. Deleting Siri history and turning off "Improve Siri" are two separate switches. Do both. |

## Cameras and doorbells

| Device | What it keeps | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ring | Videos up to 180 days with a subscription | No setting stops a legal request for video. You can turn off Public Safety emails. A video you share exposes your home address and cannot be taken back, so share rarely. |
| Nest cameras | About 3 hours without a subscription | Removing a camera from your account deletes its history. |
| Wyze | 14 days | Review the retention setting. |
| Blink | 60 days by default (choices 3, 7, 14, 30) | Clips on a USB drive stay until you delete them. With a subscription, copies go from the cloud to the drive every 24 hours. |
| Eufy | Local storage | Clear local storage from the app before disposal. |

Not covered here (look at each maker's privacy screen): Arlo, baby monitors such as Nanit
and Owlet, and Ecovacs.

## Robot vacuums

The map of your home lives in the maker's cloud. A reset using the robot's buttons leaves
that cloud data in place. Use the app's own "Factory Reset and Remove Robot" option
instead. Roborock steps were not confirmed (check first).

## Home Assistant

If you run Home Assistant, history stays on your own hardware and keeps 10 days by default.

## Still open for each device

Look at the app's list of people who can view or control the device, and remove anyone who
should not be there. Check that the device is on a guest or separate network (see
`network/router-hygiene/`).
