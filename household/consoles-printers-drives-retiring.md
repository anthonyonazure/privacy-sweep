# Consoles, streaming sticks, printers, drives, and retiring devices

Factory resets do not remove cloud saves, subscriptions, or the device's entry in your
account. Remove the device from the account too.

## Consoles and streaming devices

| Device | Steps |
| --- | --- |
| PlayStation 5 | Settings, System, System Software, Reset Options, Reset Your Console. PS4: Initialize PS4, then Full. |
| Xbox | Factory reset, then remove the console at account.microsoft.com/devices. |
| Nintendo Switch | Deregister from the primary console first, then run full initialization. A limit of one deregistration per year was not confirmed (check first). |
| Roku | Factory reset unlinks the account. |
| Fire TV | Reset to Factory Defaults. |
| Apple TV | Reset, then Remove from Account. The vendor page dates from 2023, and a device may need up to 90 days before joining a new account. |
| Kindle | Deregister, then reset. |
| Meta Quest | Factory reset (boot menu, or the Meta account devices page). Room maps already uploaded stay: use Settings, Privacy and safety, Device permissions, Clear physical space history. A cloud backup also survives a reset. |

PlayStation and Xbox voice chat history was not confirmed (check first).

## Printers and copiers

- **Home printer:** factory reset. On HP, Restore Factory Defaults deletes pages held in
  memory.
- **Office copier or leased machine:** it often holds a hard drive with images of what was
  copied and scanned. Deleting or reformatting does not remove the data. At the end of a
  lease, have the vendor overwrite or destroy the drive. Overwrite does not work on solid
  state drives, so those must be removed and destroyed.

## Drives and network storage

- One overwrite pass is enough. Multi-pass wiping is unnecessary and wears flash drives.
- A quick format leaves data recoverable.
- Degaussing does not work on flash storage.
- Synology and QNAP both have a Secure Erase action. QNAP also has SED Erase for
  self-encrypting drives.
- Encrypted drives can be made unreadable by destroying the key (cryptographic erase).
- USB stick and SD card wipe steps were not confirmed (check first). Physical destruction
  is the safe fallback for anything sensitive.

## Retiring a phone or computer

| Device | Steps |
| --- | --- |
| iPhone or iPad | Sign out, then Erase All Content and Settings. iCloud content stays in your account. |
| Mac | Apple silicon or T2: Erase All Content and Settings. Intel without T2: sign out, then reinstall macOS. |
| Pixel | Remove your Google account first. Its backup is deleted with the account. |
| Samsung | Factory data reset. Remove the Google and Samsung accounts first. A reset done outside Settings triggers factory reset protection. |
| Windows 11 | Settings, Reset this PC, with Clean data. This does not meet government erasure standards. Remove the PC at account.microsoft.com/devices. Keep your BitLocker key until the reset is done. |
